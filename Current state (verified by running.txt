Settlements. Here's why it beats the others as the next move:

It's a prerequisite for two things already flagged as known bugs in your notes (settlement hysteresis, safety-in-numbers) — building those fixes now would be fixing something that doesn't exist yet.
Wilderness hazard is much more meaningful once there's an "outside a settlement" to be a hazard relative to — right now every tile is equivalent, so a hazard system would just be uniform extra death, not the "learn that wandering is dangerous" behavior your spec wants.
Resolution promotion needs triggers like "became a local leader" or "joined a settlement's council" — most of the promotion triggers in your spec assume settlements exist.
Bottleneck diagnostics get much more useful once there's a settlement layer to diagnose (e.g. "settlement X collapsed because Y"), rather than just a flat tile grid.
